# Glossary

## AI analysis

Structured machine-generated assessment of a learner submission against task context and an evaluation reference.

## AI-native

AI is part of the central product workflow and data model rather than an unrelated chat widget. This term must not imply autonomous authority.

## Answer key

Tutor-provided expected answer or acceptable variants. Must remain hidden from learners unless intentionally released.

## Assignment

A tutor-created unit of work containing one or more tasks and optionally a deadline, materials and evaluation references.

## Attempt

One learner work instance for an assignment version.

## Confirmed progress

Progress evidence accepted or corrected by the authorized tutor according to current product policy.

## Context package

The minimal authorized information sent to an AI provider for a specific operation.

## Demo data

Synthetic information used for demonstration and testing. It is not real pilot evidence.

## Evidence

A traceable observation tied to a task, submission, test, interview or source.

## Feedback release policy

Rule determining when and which AI or tutor feedback is visible to the learner.

## Human in the loop

Tutor reviews, corrects or approves important AI output rather than delegating final authority to the model.

## Learner

The person receiving tutoring and completing assignments. May be a minor.

## Learning cycle

Assignment → learner work → analysis/feedback → tutor review → confirmed progress/next action.

## MAX Mini App

Proposed structured client surface within MAX. Exact capabilities and required format must be confirmed from official documentation.

## P0 / P1 / P2

Priority classes: essential core, pilot-value enhancement and later expansion.

## Pilot

Time-bounded real use with an adult tutor and safely managed learner participation to collect evidence and identify limitations.

## Preliminary AI feedback

AI-generated information not yet approved by a tutor.

## Progress snapshot

A derived summary of learner evidence. It must not replace the underlying evidence trail.

## Prompt version

Versioned identifier for the AI instruction/template used to generate an analysis.

## Rubric

Tutor-defined or approved criteria used to evaluate a response.

## Skill

A versioned element of the subject taxonomy used to classify evidence and gaps.

## Skill-gap candidate

An AI-proposed gap tied to evidence. It is not automatically confirmed learner truth.

## Tutor

An independent adult educator using reprep to manage and support learners.

## Tutor review

Tutor action that confirms, corrects or rejects AI analysis.

## Workspace

Proposed authorization boundary containing one tutor's relationships and tutor-owned resources.
